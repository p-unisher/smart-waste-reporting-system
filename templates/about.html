About Our System

The Smart Waste Reporting System is designed to help citizens report waste and garbage problems in their area.


Users can submit information about waste by providing the location, description and photo. The submitted report can then be reviewed by the administrator.


Users can also track the status of their report and know whether action has been taken.


The main aim of this system is to encourage people to participate in keeping their city clean and create a better environment.
