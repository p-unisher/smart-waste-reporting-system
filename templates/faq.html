Frequently Asked Questions

1. How can I report waste?

Go to the Report Waste page and enter the required details about the waste. You can also upload a photo.

2. Can I track my report?

Yes. After submitting a report, you can use your Report ID on the Track Status page.

3. What information is required?

You need to provide the waste type, location and description. A waste photo can also be uploaded.

4. Who reviews the reports?

The administrator reviews the submitted reports and updates their status.

5. What happens after I submit a report?

The report will be saved in the system. The administrator can review it and take the required action.
